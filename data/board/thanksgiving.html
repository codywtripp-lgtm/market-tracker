Questions shoppers ask

When is the best time to buy food for Thanksgiving?

Usually Thanksgiving week for whole turkey, sweet potatoes, cranberries, butter, spiral ham, cream cheese, celery, Brussels sprouts and mandarins: supermarkets run holiday specials on them then, and prices drop well below where they are now. Green beans, mushrooms and pears usually go the other way, so buy them when you see a good price.

Is Thanksgiving food more expensive this year?

Right now, cranberries, butter, spiral ham, eggs, carrots, apples, oranges, mandarins and bagged salad are cheaper than usual for this time of year. Sweet potatoes, celery, Brussels sprouts, green beans, onions, mushrooms and garlic are pricier than usual.

Why are sweet potatoes so cheap at Thanksgiving?

Stores use holiday staples as specials to bring shoppers in, so many more stores feature them that week. In past years, the number of stores running specials Thanksgiving week was about 6 times higher for sweet potatoes, 5 times higher for Brussels sprouts and 8 times higher for cranberries than at this point in the fall.
