Questions shoppers ask

When is the best time to buy produce for Thanksgiving?

For sweet potatoes, cranberries, celery and Brussels sprouts, the week before Thanksgiving: supermarkets put them on sale then, and prices usually drop well below late-October levels. Green beans and mushrooms usually go the other way, so buy them when you see a good sale.

Are Thanksgiving vegetables more expensive this year?

Right now, cranberries, carrots, apples, oranges, mandarins and bagged salad are cheaper than usual for this time of year. Sweet potatoes, celery, Brussels sprouts, green beans, onions, mushrooms and garlic are pricier than usual.

Why are sweet potatoes and cranberries so cheap at Thanksgiving?

Stores use them as holiday specials to bring shoppers in, so many more stores put them on sale that week; in past years six to eight times as many stores ran sales on them as in late October.
