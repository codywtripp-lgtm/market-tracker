Questions buyers ask

What is the wholesale price of choice cutout today?

$360.79 per cwt ($3.61 a pound) on USDA's latest report, November 6.

Is that high for the time of year?

High for the season. Usually $282.62 to $328.59 at this time of year, in today's dollars.

Will the price go up or down?

Steady: about $356 per cwt in two weeks.

Where does this price come from?

USDA's national boxed beef report: negotiated sales, FOB plant, for delivery within 21 days. Packers' formula and forward sales aren't in these prices. USDA reports no city prices for boxed beef.
