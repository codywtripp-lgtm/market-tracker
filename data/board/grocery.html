How this works

Quality is what to expect this time of year: new crop and peak harvest rate excellent; storage, imports, end-of-season and growing-area changeovers rate fair or poor. Tap or hover over a rating for the reason. A low price on poor-quality produce is never listed as a best buy.

Every week USDA records the produce prices supermarkets advertise, about 9,772 stores for the most-advertised items. We compare this week’s average ad price with the same weeks in the last five to ten years, adjusted for inflation: the cheapest quarter of those weeks is a great price, the highest quarter is pricey. “In season” is from USDA’s seasonal produce guide. These are advertised sale prices; your store’s shelf price may differ.
