How this works

Every week USDA records the meat and poultry prices U.S. supermarkets advertise, from about 27,500 stores for the most common items. We compare this week’s average price with the same weeks in the last five years, adjusted for inflation: the cheapest quarter of those weeks is a great price, the highest quarter is pricey. These are advertised prices; your store’s everyday shelf price may differ.
