Questions shoppers ask

How much do watermelons cost at the grocery store?

Watermelons average $3.98 each at U.S. supermarkets this week, 16% below usual for mid November.

Are watermelons in season right now?

Watermelons are out of season now; they’re in season in summer. Prices are lowest in October, November and December, but that’s out-of-season produce; for the best quality, buy in June, July and August.

When are watermelons cheapest?

In past years, supermarket prices for watermelons were lowest in October, November and December.
