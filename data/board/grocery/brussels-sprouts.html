How much do brussels sprouts cost at the grocery store?

Are brussels sprouts in season right now?

Brussels sprouts are in season now. Sale prices are lowest in February, July and August, but that’s out-of-season produce; for the best quality, buy in September, October, November and December.

When are brussels sprouts cheapest?

In past years, supermarket sale prices for brussels sprouts were lowest in February, July and August, and they were most often on sale in February, July and December.
