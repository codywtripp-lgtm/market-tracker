How much do honeydews cost at the grocery store?

Honeydews average $3.44 each at U.S. supermarkets this week, 17% below usual for mid November.

Are honeydews in season right now?

Honeydews are out of season now; they’re in season in summer. Prices are lowest in April, May and June, but that’s out-of-season produce; for the best quality, buy in July, August, September and October.
