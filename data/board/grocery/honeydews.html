There’s no U.S. supermarket price for honeydews this week. The last one, the week of November 20, was $3.50 each. Honeydews are out of season now; they’re in season in summer. When to buy

Usually cheapest in May, at $3.90 each, and highest in September, at $4.66. November usually runs $4.13.
