How much are apricots this week?

Supermarket ads across the U.S., week of October 23, 2026, from USDA

Apricots are not in this week’s supermarket ads. Stores last advertised them the week of July 17 at $3.99/lb. Apricots are out of season now; they’re in season in spring and summer. They’re usually cheapest in February, July and August.
