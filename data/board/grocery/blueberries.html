Questions shoppers ask

How much do blueberries cost at the grocery store?

Blueberries average $4.94 for 1 pint at U.S. supermarkets this week, 48% above usual for mid November.

Are blueberries in season right now?

Blueberries are out of season now; they’re in season in summer.

Why are blueberries so expensive right now?

Shipments are 41% below last year and wholesale prices at Hunts Point are 27% above usual, according to this week’s USDA reports.

How much more do organic blueberries cost?

Organic blueberries averaged $6.00 for 1 pint in this week’s store flyers, 21% more than regular (453 stores advertised it).

When are blueberries cheapest?

In past years, supermarket prices for blueberries were lowest in June, July and August.
