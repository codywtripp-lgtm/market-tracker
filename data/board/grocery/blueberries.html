Questions shoppers ask

How much do blueberries cost at the grocery store?

Blueberries average $4.76 for 1 pint at U.S. supermarkets this week, 31% above usual for early November.

Are blueberries in season right now?

Blueberries are out of season now; they’re in season in summer.

Why are blueberries so expensive right now?

Shipments are 15% below last year and wholesale prices at Hunts Point are 29% above usual, according to this week’s USDA reports.

When are blueberries cheapest?

In past years, supermarket prices for blueberries were lowest in June, July and August.
