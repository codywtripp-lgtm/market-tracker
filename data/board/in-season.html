How this works

“In season” follows USDA’s SNAP-Ed Seasonal Produce Guide. Quality is what to expect this month: new crop and peak harvest rate excellent; storage, imports and the end of a season rate fair or poor. Prices are the average U.S. supermarkets advertised this week, reported by USDA; your store’s price may differ. Fruits and vegetables are grouped the way USDA’s MyPlate groups them, so avocados and tomatoes count as vegetables.
