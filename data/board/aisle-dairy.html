How this works

Every week USDA records the dairy and eggs prices U.S. supermarkets advertise, from about 16,000 stores for the most common items. We compare this week’s average price with the same weeks in the last five years, adjusted for inflation: the cheapest quarter of those weeks is a great price, the highest quarter is pricey. The two-week outlook comes from a model of each item’s seasonal pattern and how far it is from its usual price. In testing on 2022 to 2026 prices, the price moved the way the outlook said (by 5% or more) 77% of the time. These are advertised prices; your store’s everyday shelf price may differ.
